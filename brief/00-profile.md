# Profile: source of truth

Status: unfilled

Claude: interview the user with the questions under each heading, a section at a
time, and replace the questions with what they say. Three habits make this file
work:

- **Quote them.** Their sentence about what drained them at the last job screens
  better than your summary of it. Keep the quote and add the rule it implies.
- **Name examples.** Every preference gets a real company, product, or posting
  that shows it, and one that shows the opposite.
- **Date changes.** When they change their mind, write "Set on YYYY-MM-DD" and
  what it replaced. The search doubles as finding out what they want.

Don't block on a question they can't answer yet. Write the assumption, label it
as one, and move on.

## Identity

- What name goes on applications?
- Where are you based, and in what time zone?
- Where are you authorised to work?
- In one sentence, what is this search for? Name what you're optimising for
  (energy, pay, learning, time with family, a specific kind of work) and what is
  only a constraint.

## Location and relocation

- Is remote the default, a requirement, or a preference?
- Which cities or regions count in a role's favour?
- Would you relocate, and for what?
- Is travel a plus, a cost, or a hard no?
- Should location ever cut a company, or only show on the card?

## Compensation

- What pay are you coming from, and what's the target range?
- What's the floor for accepting an offer, and where does it come from (a
  household budget, a prior salary)?
- Is there an exception, a lower number a dream job could go to? What makes a
  job that kind of job?

## Level

- What level and shape of role do you want (IC or manager, reports or none)?
- Does any level cost you something to perform, and what?

## What drains you

One row per drain, with the condition that makes it a drain and the condition
that makes the same thing fine. "Meetings" is too coarse; "meetings that produce
no decision" screens.

## What gives you energy

- Which pieces of work left you with more energy than you started with? List
  them, then say what they have in common.
- What's the unit of work you like to finish (a page, a fix, a customer
  onboarded, a video)?
- Does live work with other people give energy or take it, and under what
  structure?

Turn the answers into rules for role selection, such as "favour roles whose unit
of work is finishable in days".

## What went wrong before

One paragraph per past job that ended badly or wore you down. What was the plan,
what happened instead, and what would you screen for now to avoid it? These
become the red flags in `01-criteria.md`.

## The core drive

Is there one thing you've done since you were a kid that explains your best work?
Describe it, then list the work results it produced. Roles get tested against it.

## Target lanes

A lane is a kind of role worth sourcing. For each one:

- The work, in a sentence, and why it fits the energy answers above.
- **The test** that separates the real version of this role from the lookalike
  with the same title.
- **Titles to source.**
- **Hard filters** inside the lane (a required language, a stack, an on-call
  shape).
- An example posting that fits and one that looks close and doesn't.

Order the lanes by preference, and say which stay open only because the search
has to make money.

## The customer you want to serve

Who do you want the company's customers to be, and who can't they be (enterprise
procurement, developers, consumers)? This becomes a hard filter.

## Portfolio and capability

- What can you show: repos, published work, videos, sites, metrics with sources?
- Where does it live (paths and URLs)?
- Which one piece do you point to first, and what does it prove?
- What do you do by hand, and what do you only do with AI? Applications must
  never claim the second as the first.

## Products you love

List them, then name the thread they share. Add the domains that follow from the
list. Research sources companies from both.

## Constraints

- Any schedule needs?
- Any companies or industries excluded? Any you have history with?
- How long is the runway, and does a date change what a good outcome is?
