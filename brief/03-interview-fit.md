# Interview process fit

Status: unfilled

A company can score well on everything else and still be unreachable when its
hiring process measures the user poorly. Every tier 3 deep dive maps the process
step by step and grades it against this file.

Claude: ask the questions, keep the user's own words, then write the grading
scale from their answers.

## What doesn't work

Which interview formats under-measure you? Behavioural questions, live
whiteboard coding, take-homes on the company's own product, panels, long onsite
loops. For each, say whether it's a penalty or a reason not to apply.

## What works

Which formats show what you can do? Portfolio review, a work sample on an open
project, pairing on a real problem, a recorded demo. Name the evidence you
already have that each one would use.
