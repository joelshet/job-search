# Research plan

## Shape: tiered funnel

| Tier | Work per company | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Screen | Basic screening and the stage 1 hard filters. Fast pass/fail. | A card: first paragraph and case against |
| 2. Profile | Full rubric scored, sources cited. | Scores table and evidence on the company page |
| 3. Deep dive | Evidence-backed write-up, interview process mapped, specific open roles, risks named. | Interview process and next action on the company page |

The user reads cards in triage and tier 3 pages. Tiers 1 and 2 make the
shortlist defensible and keep good companies from being silently dropped. Only
companies the user labelled `Y` or `y` go to tier 2, and tier 3 only when they
ask.

## Sourcing strategy

Source beyond job boards, which surface the companies with the biggest recruiting
budgets:

- **Domain-led sourcing**, from the target domains in `00-profile.md`
- **Archetype-led sourcing:** start from the products the user loves and find
  their peers, competitors, and the companies their people leave to join
- **List-led sourcing:** a directory or database that records a fact the screen
  needs (headcount, funding, what they ship) for another reason, such as trade
  show exhibitor lists, grant award records, partner directories, and
  crowdfunding archives. Anchor each on one probed source, and use search only
  to confirm a website
- Public company handbooks and engineering blogs
- Job postings whose wording matches the profile's energy rules
- Employee-report sources for the culture claims that matter
- Layoff and return-to-office trackers
- Compensation data for the comp filter

Show the user five results from a new source before producing volume.

## What each tier 3 deep dive must contain

1. What the company does, who pays them, and whether that's durable
2. Score per rubric dimension, each with source and confidence level
3. **The interview process, mapped step by step, graded A-F** against
   `03-interview-fit.md`
4. Specific open roles matching the target lanes, with links
5. The case against joining. Every company gets one
6. What to ask, and what to verify, before applying

## Non-negotiables

- Don't block on unanswered profile fields. State an assumption, label it, and
  move on.
- No scoring a culture claim from the company's own recruiting copy.
- Every company gets a stated case against it. A dossier with no downside hasn't
  been researched.
- Commit as the run goes, so a long run never loses work.
